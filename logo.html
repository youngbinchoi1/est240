Personal Brand Mark · Youngbin Choi
About the Mark

This logo directly denotes the initial of my name, Youngbin, making it special by adding a last name as a sub-text and intertwining the stem of the letter "Y". It was created in blue color, which is inspired by the blue ocean that the theme of 'travel' represents.

How It Was Made
Prompt used to create it A monogram logo design featuring the letters "Y", enclosed within a perfect circle. The "Y" dominates the upper portion with wide serif-style strokes, while a tall curved "C" shape flows downward and curves back up, creating a unified, interlocking glyph. The stem of the "Y" is interwined. Small lowercase text "choi" appears in refined, spaced-out serif lettering to the right of center. The circle border is a thin line that frames the composition.
